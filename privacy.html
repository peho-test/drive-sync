Privacy policy

Private Drive Sync is a private, single-user setup. The only person who signs in to it is its owner.

What Google user data it accesses

With the owner's consent it accesses files and folders in the owner's own Google Drive, in order to list, upload and download them.

How the data is used and stored

Files are copied between the owner's Google Drive and computers that the owner controls. An access token is stored on the owner's own server so that scheduled backups can run. The owner alone decides what happens with the files.

Sharing

The setup has no other users and collects no data about anyone else. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with third parties for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services".

Contact

Use the support e-mail address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen of this app.
